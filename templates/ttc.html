About the Program

Vyoma's Teacher Training is built around small-group, hands-on learning rather than large lecture-style classes you'll be practicing and teaching alongside a handful of fellow students, not sitting in a crowd.

The approach stays practical throughout: less time memorizing theory, more time actually teaching, receiving feedback, and refining your voice as an instructor. Whether you're aiming to teach professionally or simply want to understand your own practice at a deeper level, the training meets you at whichever tier fits your goals and schedule.

Curriculum

Every tier builds on the same core foundation the longer programs simply go deeper into each area and add supervised teaching practice.

01

Asana & Alignment

The physical postures proper form, common modifications, and how to guide a body safely into and out of each pose.

02

Anatomy & Physiology

The basics of how the body moves, so you understand why alignment matters and how to prevent injury. (100hr & 200hr)

03

Yoga Philosophy

The history and ethical foundations behind the practice where the physical postures fit into a much older tradition.

04

Teaching Methodology

How to sequence a class, cue clearly, and adapt on the fly for different bodies and skill levels in the room. (100hr & 200hr)

05

Practicum

Supervised practice teaching in front of your fellow trainees, with real feedback before you ever lead a public class. (200hr)
