Hatha

Slow, grounded postures held with attention. Best for beginners and anyone easing back into practice.

60 minutes. Poses are held at a deliberate pace, giving you time to settle into alignment and breath before moving to the next. No previous experience needed — modifications are offered throughout.

Qi Gong photo coming soon

Qi Gong

Breath-linked movement that builds heat and strength. For students comfortable with the basics.

60 minutes. Continuous, flowing movement paired with breath, drawing on principles distinct from seated yoga practice. A gentler entry point into movement-based energy work.

Core photo coming soon

Core

Deep, passive stretches that work the connective tissue. Meditative and slow by design.

60 minutes. Poses are held far longer than in a typical flow class, targeting the deeper connective tissue rather than the muscles. Best suited to those wanting a slower, more internal practice.

Power photo coming soon

Power

A fast-paced, dynamic flow — the opposite pace of Hatha. Postures link continuously to the breath rather than being held individually, closer in feel to a cardio workout than a slow, meditative practice.

60 minutes. Best for students already comfortable with basic poses who want to build strength, stamina, and heat.

Kids Yoga

Playful, story-driven movement — poses introduced through games, animal names, and simple breathing exercises rather than formal instruction.

60 minutes. Designed for children roughly 5–10 years old. Builds focus, coordination, and emotional regulation in a lighthearted, non-competitive setting.
